Alert

Alerts display important messages that require the user's attention.

Usage

This is an informational message. Operation completed successfully. Please check your configuration. Something went wrong.
HTML 
<ds-alert title="Info">This is an informational message.</ds-alert>
<ds-alert variant="success" title="Success">Operation completed successfully.</ds-alert>
<ds-alert variant="warning" title="Warning">Please check your configuration.</ds-alert>
<ds-alert variant="danger" title="Error">Something went wrong.</ds-alert>
